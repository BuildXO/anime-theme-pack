# Background Artwork Attribution (v0.2.0 themes)

The three backgrounds added in v0.2.0 are anime-style artworks by
Liminal Games (itch.io creator page: https://liminal-space-dev.itch.io/),
each released under Creative Commons Zero v1.0 Universal (CC0 1.0),
a public domain dedication: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

CC0 places no restrictions on use, modification, or redistribution, including
committing the files to this public repository. Attribution is not legally
required; it is recorded here for provenance.

Each source store page states, under "More information":
"Asset license: Creative Commons Zero v1.0 Universal" and license terms
"Use in both commercial and free projects". License lines captured from the
store pages on 2026-11-09 (see LICENSE-EVIDENCE.md).
